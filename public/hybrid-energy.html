Coordinate solar, storage and grid supply.

A hybrid system brings generation, storage and controls together around the way your facility operates. Existing equipment must be assessed before integration.

IS THIS RIGHT FOR YOUR BUSINESS?

Hybrid Energy Systems

Who it suits

Businesses that need both energy savings and resilience, or want to expand an existing solar installation.

What the assessment examines

Existing inverters, protection, switchgear, load distribution, generator interfaces where relevant, monitoring and the equipment that must remain powered during an interruption.

What to compare

Critical versus whole-site backup, operating modes, power limits, control compatibility, commissioning tests and the responsibilities of each delivery party.

What needs validation

An integrated system is not automatically an off-grid system. The proposal must define the expected grid contribution, backup duration, operating constraints and response when storage is depleted.
