Generate electricity where your business uses it.

Solar can reduce daytime grid purchases when generation aligns with your facility’s demand. The useful output depends on the site, system design and how much generation can be consumed.

IS THIS RIGHT FOR YOUR BUSINESS?

Commercial Solar PV

Who it suits

Businesses with suitable roof or land area and meaningful daytime consumption, including manufacturing, warehousing, retail and commercial property.

What the assessment examines

Your electricity bills, interval consumption where available, tariff, operating hours, roof condition, shading, usable area and existing electrical infrastructure.

What to compare

System capacity in kWp, expected monthly generation, self-consumption, export assumptions, maintenance and the effect of remaining fixed or demand charges. Roof suitability and grid requirements need site-specific confirmation.

What solar alone does not establish

Standard grid-connected solar does not automatically provide power during an outage. Backup requires an appropriately designed system, controls and isolation arrangements.
