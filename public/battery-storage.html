IS THIS RIGHT FOR YOUR BUSINESS?

Battery Energy Storage

Who it suits

Facilities with critical equipment, evening demand, suitable time-of-use opportunities or peaks that may be managed with storage.

What the assessment examines

Critical-load power in kW, required backup duration, usable energy in kWh, starting loads, charging opportunities, inverter capability, losses and battery operating limits.

What to compare

Usable rather than nominal capacity, available power, degradation, cycle and warranty conditions, expected dispatch and maintenance. A battery’s energy capacity does not tell you the power it can supply.

What needs validation

A monthly electricity total alone cannot establish battery size or achievable independence. A high-independence target needs time-based simulation, seasonal checks and a clear definition of the target.
