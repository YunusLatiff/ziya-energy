15 / DECISION-MAKER FAQ

Questions we expect before a project starts.

Do I need to fund the entire system upfront?

No. Depending on the project, credit profile and economics, direct CAPEX, financed ownership and PPA / Energy-as-a-Service structures can be considered.

How do you size a battery system?

BESS sizing depends on the load profile, peak periods, solar production, required autonomy, critical loads, usable depth of discharge and intended cycling strategy. A monthly kWh figure alone is not enough for final design.

Can Ziya work with an existing solar system?

Yes. Existing PV can be assessed alongside current demand, inverter architecture and storage objectives to determine whether expansion or BESS integration makes sense.

How long does a commercial project take?

Timing depends on system size, site conditions, approvals, equipment availability and commercial structure. The assessment stage establishes a realistic implementation pathway for the specific facility.

What do you need to assess my site?

A recent electricity bill, monthly consumption, tariff information, operating hours and basic facility information are enough to begin. Interval data and single-line diagrams improve accuracy when available.

Can batteries reduce electricity cost as well as provide backup?

Yes. Depending on the tariff and load profile, batteries can support peak shaving, load shifting, improved solar self-consumption and resilience. The dispatch strategy determines where the value is created.
