ASSESSMENT GUIDE

A better decision starts
with better questions.

Use this guide to understand the scope of an energy assessment. It is an explanation of the process, not a quotation or a completed project case study.

01 / UNDERSTAND THE BASELINE

What does your facility need?

Gather electricity bills, tariffs, operating days and hours, and information about existing equipment. Interval consumption helps show when energy is used. List the loads that need backup and their power requirements.

02 / COMPARE LIKE WITH LIKE

What does the model assume?

A useful comparison separates solar generation, on-site consumption, battery losses and remaining grid imports. It should show monthly results and explain weather assumptions, degradation, tariff components and how equipment is operated.

ILLUSTRATIVE ARITHMETIC — NOT A SITE FORECAST

Energy reduction is not bill reduction.

If a facility uses 10,000 kWh in a month and imports 500 kWh from the grid, its grid-energy reduction relative to that usage is 95%. This calculation does not establish whether a proposed system can achieve that result, and fixed or demand charges may remain.

For a target in every month, check every modelled month rather than relying on an annual average.

03 / DEFINE THE NEXT STEP

What will be delivered,
and by whom?

Ask for the proposed equipment, scope, assumptions, expected benefits and limitations. Confirm site-validation work, approvals, design responsibility, installer, commissioning, monitoring and maintenance arrangements.

Request the scope and any fees for detailed assessment before proceeding. Timing depends on the information available and the complexity of the site.
