WEBSITE ENQUIRIES

How information is used.

When you submit an enquiry, Ziya Energy receives the contact and facility details you provide so we can respond, assess the opportunity and follow up on that enquiry. Please do not include passwords, banking information or other sensitive information in the form.

Service providers

The website uses Cloudflare for hosting and form security. Enquiry emails are processed through Resend and the receiving email service. Where configured, an enquiry record is also stored in a Cloudflare database. These providers may process information outside South Africa.

Storage and access

Submitted details are handled in the enquiry inbox and, where enabled, the lead database. Contact us to ask about retained information, request correction or request deletion. Some records may need to be retained for a project relationship or applicable record-keeping obligations.

Optional measurement

Optional visitor analytics and enquiry-interaction measurement are loaded only if you choose to allow measurement and it has been configured. You can change your choice using “Privacy choices” in the footer. Necessary security processing still applies.

Your choices

The starting-point tool keeps its answers in this browser tab temporarily so they can be carried to the enquiry form. They are not sent as an enquiry until you submit the form. Your measurement choice is stored in your browser.

WhatsApp and email links open separate services whose privacy practices also apply.

Contact

For questions or requests about your information, email yunus@ziyaenergy.co.za.
