HOW IT WORKS

Refer. Track. Earn.

01 / Create your account

Register with your name, surname and email. Verify your email using the secure sign-in link, then read the guide in your portal.

02 / Introduce a client

Ask for the client's permission before sharing their contact details. Submit the company, decision maker, location and useful project information through your workspace.

03 / Follow the opportunity

Ziya Energy reviews the referral and develops the opportunity. Your portal shows your own deals, progress updates and commission records.

THE COMMISSION

3% on completed deals.

Commission is 3% of the confirmed final deal value. It becomes earned when Ziya Energy marks the deal completed. Figures before completion are estimates; payment status is recorded separately.

For example, a completed deal with a confirmed final value of R1,000,000 earns R30,000 in commission. This is an example of the calculation, not a promise of earnings or a project forecast.

Ziya reviews client attribution and project suitability. Funding, project scope, equipment, savings and timing need the relevant assessment and written proposal.

YOUR PRIVATE WORKSPACE

You can see the clients you submit, their deal stages, shared updates and commission status. The Ziya Energy administrator can review every referral; other affiliates cannot view your deals.
