What still needs power at your business after sunset?

Your monthly electricity bill is a starting point. A night-load list helps explain where the energy is needed.

Swipe for the checklist, then record:
1. The equipment and its running power.
2. When it operates and for how long.
3. Which loads run at the same time.

Refrigeration, security and essential lighting are examples—not a ready-made list for every site. Motors may also need extra power to start, and cycling equipment does not necessarily draw its rated power all night.

Battery energy capacity (kWh) and the power needed at one time (kW) answer different questions. Usable capacity, losses, controls and the charging plan also matter. Measured load data helps turn the list into a suitable design.

Save this for your next energy assessment, or share it with the person responsible for your facility.
