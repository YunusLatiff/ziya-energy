Make an introduction. Keep track of what happens next.

Ziya Energy’s affiliate portal gives you a private view of the clients you refer, their deal stages and commission records. Other affiliates cannot see your deals; Ziya Energy’s administrator reviews all referrals.

Start here:
1. Create your account and verify your email.
2. Ask the client’s permission before sharing their details.
3. Submit the referral and follow its progress in your workspace.

Commission is 3% of the confirmed final deal value, earned when Ziya Energy marks the deal completed. Figures before completion are estimates. Payment status is recorded separately.

A useful introduction starts with a business or property owner exploring solar or battery storage. Ziya assesses project suitability; a referral does not guarantee a completed deal or earnings.
