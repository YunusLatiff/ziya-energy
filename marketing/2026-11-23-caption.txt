Before accepting a solar quote, ask these three questions.

1. What is included?
Ask for the equipment, installation, electrical protection, commissioning and any backup circuits to be listed in writing. Check warranty and after-sales responsibilities too.

2. What costs extra?
Confirm VAT, exclusions, additional electrical work and any recurring charges. Compare complete scopes—not just the headline total.

3. What supports the savings estimate?
Ask which load profile, tariff, solar-production assumptions and operating strategy were used. Expected savings need a calculation, and actual results depend on the site and system.
