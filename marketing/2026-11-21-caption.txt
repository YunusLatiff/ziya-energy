Solar panels and backup power do different jobs.

☀️ Solar panels generate electricity from sunlight.
🔋 Batteries store energy for later use.

A conventional grid-tied solar system may shut down during a grid outage—even when the sun is shining. Backup needs the right inverter, controls, wiring and system design.

Before choosing equipment, define the job:
• Which loads must stay on?
• How much power do they need at once?
• For how long?
• When does your business use the most energy?

Start with those answers, then size the system around your operating profile.
